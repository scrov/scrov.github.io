# EJS Templates

Use EJS for server-side view composition when a server-rendered template is the right adapter.

```ejs
<section class="terminal-card">
  <h1><%= profile.title %></h1>
  <p><%= profile.summary %></p>
</section>
```

Keep business decisions outside the template. Views receive already-shaped data and render it.
